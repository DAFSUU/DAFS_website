How the EU talks about the green transition

Research question

How has the EU’s discussion of the green transition shifted between environmental protection, competitiveness and security?

Short answer

    How we measured it

    The European Parliament is where the EU debates in public: Commission proposals on climate and energy are argued over there, by members from every member state and political family. We read every plenary speech by an MEP from July 2009 to September 2023, in an English version (the original or a machine translation), and measured how each sentence about the green transition is framed.

    Does the answer depend on the selection?

    The reader check found that some selected speeches, mostly near the threshold, are about energy prices, budgets or pollution rather than the transition itself, and those speeches use more security language. So the main comparison is repeated with stricter selections. The direction of every finding holds; the size of the security turn depends on whether debates on energy prices and supply count as part of the green-transition debate.
